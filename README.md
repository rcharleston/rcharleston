## A little about my work

My experience spans GRC, security engineering, cloud posture assessment, and SOC alert triage across financial services, hospitality, and state government. I enjoy the point where technical detail becomes a decision someone can act on: a finding with evidence, a policy with a clear purpose, or a repeatable reporting workflow.

Professional work stays private. These repositories contain learning labs, sanitized evidence, and writing I can share and discuss openly.
